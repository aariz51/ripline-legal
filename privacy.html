Privacy policy

Last updated: 7 October 2026

The short version

Ripline has no account and no server of its own. Your program, sessions and measurements are stored on your phone. We do not sell data, show ads, or use advertising trackers.

What stays on your phone

  • Your answers from the setup questions (goal, schedule, equipment, starting point).
  • Your sessions, ratings and the doses the program sets.
  • Measurements you log: weight, waist, neck and hips, and the body-fat estimate worked out from them.

None of this is sent to us. It is removed when you delete the app. You can export it as text from Settings.

Apple Health (iPhone, optional)

If you connect Apple Health, Ripline saves your sessions as workouts and saves the weight and waist measurements you log. It reads your latest weight and waist so you don't type them twice. Health data is read and written on your device only. It is never sent to us or to anyone else, and it is never used for advertising. You can turn this off in Settings or in the Health app.

Purchases

Subscriptions are handled by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We use RevenueCat to check whether a subscription is active. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID and your purchase receipt. It does not receive your name or email from us. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

Reminders

Training-day reminders are scheduled on your phone. No notification service or server is involved.

Analytics

This version of Ripline sends no analytics. If that changes, this policy and the store privacy labels will be updated first, and no measurements or health data will ever be included.

Children

Ripline is meant for adults and is not directed at children under 13.

Your choices

You can export or delete your data at any time: export from Settings, delete by removing the app. Because we hold no copy, there is nothing for us to delete on our side. For questions, email us at the address below.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above will change and the new version will be posted here.
