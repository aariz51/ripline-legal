Restore a subscription

On the subscription screen, or in Settings, tap Restore purchases. Use the same Apple ID or Google account you bought with.

Cancel a subscription

iPhone: Settings > your name > Subscriptions. Android: Google Play > Profile > Payments & subscriptions. Cancel at least 24 hours before renewal.

Move to a new phone

Ripline keeps your data on your phone. Export it from Settings > Your data before you switch. Restore purchases on the new phone to unlock it.

How the reveal estimate works

Body fat uses the US Navy circumference formula: waist and neck, plus hips for the female formula. The reveal range is the "athletes" band on the American Council on Exercise chart: 6–13% for men, 14–20% for women. The window assumes 0.5–1% of body weight lost a week until you have logged your own trend. It is an estimate, not medical advice.
